Signal Pattern

Some tasks are easier with a clear sequence. Offer a first step, a written note, or a shared start if it is welcome.

The gap is often between knowing and doing, not caring and not caring. Tasks can fail at initiation, sequencing, switching, prioritizing, remembering the next step, or tolerating the task demand around the task. Scaffolding, body-doubling, written steps, visible lists, prepared defaults, and tiny starts matter because they reduce launch cost.
